**What this shows, plainly:**
1. **On series N, the votes do not point at the result.** A 0-vote number came up as often as a 1-vote number (0.98% vs 0.97%). The 3-vote numbers, the ones FINAL 4 is built from, never came up once in 154 tries. In the other series, more votes meant a slightly higher rate. In N they don't.
2. **FINAL 4 on N is at chance:** 11 of 234 (4.7%) against 4% for any four numbers. The 7.5% for all series comes from the older series, and the script was tuned on those, so that figure is in-sample.
3. **44% of N results (103 of 234) had 0 votes.** No ranking of the voting lists can put them in FINAL 4, so on N about 44 rounds in 100 are lost before any ordering happens.
4. Widening does not help: top 8, 12 and 20 are all within a point or two of chance.

**What I am doing about it:** no script change. Each repair this session (N209, N218, N225, N235) fixed OCJP or cell coverage, and those are now 1041/1041. That coverage is real, but it does not choose between numbers. FINAL 4 tuned only to make the next miss a hit would fit noise. Earlier tests (weightings, tie-breaks, the step/flip rules, the digit study) all came out at chance out of sample. I can't promise FINAL 4 will hit more often, because the N results so far behave like random draws against every list we have.
