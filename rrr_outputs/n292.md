**What the diagnosis shows:**
1. **Three lists beat chance in both datasets:** 3RRR RESULT, STRONG 20 and RECOMMENDED. These carry the real signal.
2. **WIDE and yx are below chance in both datasets.** A number in WIDE or yx comes *less* often than an average number. Yet both gave a FINAL 4 vote, so they pushed weaker numbers up.
3. 2-vote numbers come 1.8× more often than average, so the voting itself works. It is just weighed down by the two weak lists.
4. 44% of results still have 0 votes, and no ranking can reach those.

**Fix tested** (three fixed variants, every split, `rrr_final4/vote_lists_test.py`):

| FINAL 4 variant | Old all series | Old 1st half | Old 2nd half | Old series N | **Live N235+** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| shipped (6 voting lists) | 78 (7.5%) | 47 (9.0%) | 31 (6.0%) | 11 (4.7%) | 4 (7.0%) |
| **without WIDE and yx** | **83 (8.0%)** | **47 (9.0%)** | **36 (6.9%)** | **11 (4.7%)** | **4 (7.0%)** |
| without WIDE and yx, STRONG votes | 85 (8.2%) | 53 (10.2%) | 32 (6.1%) | 10 (4.3%) | 4 (7.0%) |
| 6 lists + STRONG votes | 78 (7.5%) | 50 (9.6%) | 28 (5.4%) | 9 (3.8%) | 4 (7.0%) |

**Shipped: WIDE and yx no longer vote for FINAL 4.** This variant is never worse than the old FINAL 4 in any column and gains 5 hits overall. The STRONG-vote variants were rejected because they lose on series N. I reran the script on all 57 live rounds: its FINAL 4 matches the test exactly, and the 4 live hits (N237 58, N257 51, N263 65, N271 97) are all kept.

**Honest size of the gain:** +0.5 point (8.0% vs 7.5% on the replay). That is real but small, and part of it may be luck. FINAL 4 will still miss about 92 rounds in 100. WIDE and yx stay on the page as lists; they just don't vote.
